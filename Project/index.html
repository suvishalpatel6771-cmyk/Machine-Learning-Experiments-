CarbonWise demonstrates how machine learning can make environmental activity data easier to explore. It is designed as an academic prototype rather than a certified emissions calculator.

Data transparency

The current training dataset is synthetic and reproducible. Its coefficients are illustrative rather than official emission factors. Real-world deployment would require validated local measurements, documented emission factors and independent evaluation.
